THE KUNFU PANDA

The panda is a cute, gentle, and beautiful animal. It is mainly found in China and lives in forests with plenty of bamboo. The panda has a black-and-white body, round face, small ears, and black patches around its eyes. It looks very adorable and attracts many people with its playful nature. Pandas mainly eat bamboo leaves and stems, but they may also eat other foods. They spend many hours eating and resting every day. Pandas are good climbers and can climb trees to find a safe place to rest. Baby pandas are very small, cute, and helpless when they are born.
